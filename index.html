Hi, I am Linh.

I study International Economics at Foreign Trade University in Hanoi, but I spent three years of high school on the Literature track. The habit of slow, attentive reading stayed with me.

Outside of class and midterms, I dance Latin competitively, explore e-commerce projects on Amazon, and collect books that make me stop and think. This journal is where I write down what stays after the last page.
